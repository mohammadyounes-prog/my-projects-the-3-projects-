About QuestAI

QuestAI leverages advanced AI models to help educators, trainers, and content creators quickly generate diverse question types tailored to specific topics, difficulty levels, and audience types.

Our platform supports multiple-choice and open-ended questions, with customizable parameters to ensure relevance and quality. Easily manage your generated questions, track their status, and export them for use in your assessments or learning materials.

How to Use
  • Generate Questions: Navigate to the "Generate Questions" page, fill in the required details like topic context, audience type, and desired number of questions, then click "Generate".
  • Review & Manage: After generation, review the questions. You can approve, reject, edit, or delete them. Approved questions are ready for use.
  • Retrieve Questions: Use the "Generate Questions" page to search, filter, and export your approved questions.
  • Task History: Keep track of all your generation tasks and their statuses.
  • Profile & Settings: Manage your account and access administrative settings if you have the necessary permissions.
