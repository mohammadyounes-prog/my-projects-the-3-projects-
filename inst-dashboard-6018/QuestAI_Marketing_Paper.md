## Design Suggestions for QuestAI Marketing Paper

### **Overall Aesthetic & Brand Identity:**
*   **Modern & Clean:** Avoid clutter. Use ample white space.
*   **Professional yet Approachable:** Balance corporate professionalism with elements that suggest user-friendliness and innovation.
*   **Tech-Forward & Educational:** Reflect both the AI aspect and the educational purpose.

### **Color Palette (Primary Suggestion):**
Base this on the QuestAI logo color (`#CC0000` - a darker red) and integrate complementary colors.

*   **Primary Accent:** `#CC0000` (Dark Red - from QuestAI logo). Use for headlines, key bullet points, call-to-action buttons.
*   **Secondary Accent:** A vibrant, intelligent blue or teal. (e.g., `#007bff` or a slightly deeper teal like `#007B80`). This conveys technology, intelligence, and trustworthiness. Use for sub-headlines, icons, or specific feature highlights.
*   **Neutral Palette:**
    *   **Backgrounds:** Off-white or light grey (`#f8f9fa` or `#f2f2f2`) for readability.
    *   **Text:** Dark charcoal grey (`#333333`) for body text – softer than pure black.
*   **Optional Tertiary Accent (for highlights/infographics):** A subtle, light orange or gold (`#ffc107` - from `page-header-orange` in `index.html`) to add warmth and energy, perhaps for small graphic elements or progress indicators.

### **Typography:**
Choose fonts that are clean, modern, and highly readable, especially given the dual-language requirement.

*   **Headlines (English & Arabic):** A bold, sans-serif font (e.g., Montserrat, Lato Black, Open Sans Bold). Use a larger size for impact.
*   **Sub-headlines (English & Arabic):** A slightly lighter weight of the headline font or another clean sans-serif (e.g., Lato Bold, Open Sans Semibold).
*   **Body Text (English):** A clear, legible sans-serif font (e.g., Open Sans, Lato, Roboto).
*   **Body Text (Arabic):** A modern, clear Arabic font that pairs well with the chosen English font (e.g., Cairo, Noto Sans Arabic, Tajawal). Ensure good readability.

### **Layout & Structure:**

#### **Page 1 (English & Arabic Sections Side-by-Side or Alternating):**
*   **Top:** Prominent QuestAI logo.
*   **Large Headline:** Center-aligned or left-aligned, using primary accent color and bold font.
*   **Sub-headline:** Below the main headline, using secondary accent color.
*   **Visual Element:** The suggested "futuristic graphic" (AI + education) should be central, perhaps bleeding into the background or occupying a significant portion of the page.
*   **Introduction Text:** Concise and impactful, below the graphic.
*   **"Transformative. Intuitive. Empowering." / Arabic equivalent:** Bold, possibly in a unique font style or background block.
*   **"Why QuestAI?" / Arabic equivalent:** Highlighted section with bullet points, using icons for each point (see Imagery).
    *   Each bullet point should be concise, leveraging the "big attractive words" concept.
*   **Dual Language Approach:**
    *   **Option A (Side-by-Side):** English on left, Arabic on right for each section/block. Requires careful layout for balance.
    *   **Option B (Alternating Blocks):** English Introduction, then Arabic Introduction, then English "Why QuestAI?", then Arabic "Why QuestAI?". This might be easier to layout.
    *   **Option C (Dedicated Pages):** Page 1 English, Page 2 Arabic (then vice-versa for the second sheet). This is simplest if it's a two-sheet handout. Given the "2-page paper" request, this seems most likely if printing front-and-back. If it's a folded brochure, side-by-side might work. *For the purpose of my text output, I've treated them as separate blocks.*

#### **Page 2 (English & Arabic Sections):**
*   **Top:** Smaller QuestAI logo. Maybe a subtle header with the page title.
*   **"Key Features..." / Arabic equivalent:** Prominent heading.
    *   Use clear, bold subheadings for each feature (e.g., "1. Intelligent AI Question Generation").
    *   Bullet points or short paragraphs for explanations. Consider using icons next to each feature title.
*   **"What You (and Your School) Will Gain:" / Arabic equivalent:** Another prominent heading.
    *   Separate sections for each stakeholder (Managers, Teachers, Students).
    *   Use bullet points for benefits.
*   **Call to Action:** Clear and concise, using the primary accent color or a distinct background block. Include placeholders for your website/contact info.
*   **Visual Element:** Could be smaller icons related to each feature/benefit, or a subtle background pattern.

### **Imagery & Icons:**
*   **Professional Stock Photos:** Use high-quality, diverse, and inclusive images of students, teachers, and school environments that convey learning, collaboration, and technology use.
*   **Flat/Line Icons:** Use a consistent style of icons for bullet points under "Why QuestAI?" and next to each "Key Feature" to break up text and add visual interest.
    *   Examples: lightbulb for "Insight," gears/cogs for "Efficiency," target for "Precision," shield/badge for "ABET/Accreditation."
*   **Data Visualization:** Subtle, clean infographic elements could be used to hint at "actionable insights" or "reporting."

### **Specific Notes for Bilingual Design:**
*   **Directionality:** Ensure Arabic text flows from right-to-left. Layout should respect this. If placing English and Arabic side-by-side, English might be left-aligned on the left half, and Arabic right-aligned on the right half.
*   **Font Pairing:** Test English and Arabic font pairs to ensure they look harmonious together.
*   **White Space:** Crucial for readability in both languages, especially with denser text.
